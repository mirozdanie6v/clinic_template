# Client Strategy Pack v1

Shared strategic source for Commercial Proposal, Mini App and Landing.

## Principle
Research is collected once, normalized into the Client Passport, analysed once and reused by every product surface. Proposal, Mini App and Landing must not independently invent positioning, pains, proof or visual language.

## Production pipeline

1. **Client Research / Data Ingestion** — website, social, maps, reviews, catalog, specialists, prices, locations, external systems, competitors.
2. **Truth Layer / Client Passport** — normalize facts, provenance, confidence, conflicts and unknowns.
3. **Business & Market Analysis** — business model, category, competitive context, strengths, weaknesses, growth opportunities, commercial priorities.
4. **Audience & Psychology Analysis** — segments, pains, fears, desired outcomes, objections, trust triggers, decision criteria, emotional state.
5. **Offer Architecture** — entry offers, strategic services, bundles, next-best-action, audience-to-offer mapping.
6. **Trust Architecture** — required proof, expert signals, reviews/results/technology/conditions and where each proof belongs in the journey.
7. **Brand Diagnosis & Positioning** — preserve, improve, remove; target perception and positioning.
8. **Product Strategy & User Journey** — jobs-to-be-done, screens, capabilities, conversion paths, booking/AI/specialist/trust placement.
9. **Messaging & Content Strategy** — tone, vocabulary, headlines, service copy, specialist presentation, CTA system, AI voice.
10. **Ideal Visual Brand System** — an improved reference version of the brand derived from its identity, category, audience psychology and strongest existing signals.
11. **Design System Contract** — spacing, typography, grids, containers, radii, controls, states, responsive and safe-area rules.
12. **Image Production Plan** — unique semantic image concept for every service group; common art direction, palette, lighting and crop rules.
13. **Asset Production** — generate/edit the full visual set according to the ideal system. Existing client imagery is reference/evidence and is used directly only when explicitly approved.
14. **Mini App / Landing Assembly** — assemble from Passport + Strategy Pack + approved assets.
15. **Design Compliance Gate** — blocking visual/design-system check.
16. **Product QA & UX Hardening** — blocking functional/integrated-product check.
17. **Runtime Acceptance** — real browser/device/WebView/integration acceptance.
18. **Release + Post-deploy Smoke**.
19. **Analytics & Learning Loop** — observe funnel, convert defects and successful patterns into reusable rules/components.

## Shared analysis rule
The business/psychology reasoning used for commercial proposals is the analytical foundation for this pack. Product-specific layers extend it with user journey, capabilities, design system, assets, runtime behaviour and analytics.

## Truth rule
Every strategic statement must be classified as one of: `fact`, `inference`, `hypothesis`, `decision`. Facts require provenance. Hypotheses must never be rendered to end users as facts.

## Visual rule
The visual target is the client's **ideal coherent brand expression**, not a literal copy of inconsistent current social feeds. The system may improve composition, styling, lighting and imagery while preserving recognizable brand anchors.

## Service imagery rule
- Every service group has a distinct image concept and distinct primary asset.
- Image semantics must match the service meaning.
- Reuse of the same primary image across different groups is blocked unless an explicit exception is documented.
- All generated images follow one declared art direction, palette, lighting logic and photographic treatment.
- Generated people cannot impersonate real specialists.
- Generated results/reviews/branch interiors cannot be presented as documentary evidence.

## Outputs
The Client Passport references the Strategy Pack documents. CI validates their presence and baseline structure before a client can progress to production assembly.
